# ATOM AI Role, Innovation, and Improvement Authority Freeze — Amendment 1D

**Decision ID:** `ATOM-AI-ROLE-AUTHORITY-FREEZE-1D`  
**Status:** FROZEN ON OWNER-APPROVED MERGE  
**Effectivity:** Before merge this text grants no authority. At its Owner-approved merge commit it becomes controlling governance law.  
**Author:** ChatGPT Pro, ATOM Chief Architect and Freeze Custodian  
**Amends:** `ATOM-AI-ROLE-AUTHORITY-FREEZE-1`, Amendments `1A`, `1B`, and `1C`  
**Technical authority:** none by itself

## 0. Owner objective

The Owner requires ATOM execution to continue without waiting for ChatGPT Pro or Codex when an implementation, repair, merge, deployment, migration, activation, or verification action is already authorized by controlling law. Claude is therefore granted autonomous execution authority inside an already-frozen boundary.

This amendment does not grant Claude independent freeze authorship or authority to widen a technical boundary.

## 1. Autonomous execution authority

When an Owner-approved controlling freeze, amendment, phase, or job card already authorizes the work, Claude may independently perform the complete execution chain without additional ChatGPT Pro approval between steps:

`create -> implement -> test -> fix -> repair -> assemble -> commit -> open PR -> resolve review findings -> merge -> apply authorized migration -> deploy/redeploy/restart -> activate authorized gates -> verify -> receipt`

Claude may continue through successive implementation and operational steps within that same frozen job or phase without waiting for a new ChatGPT Pro instruction merely because the next step is merge, deployment, repair, restart, migration application, activation, or verification.

Claude may use Codex as an implementation resource when available, but Codex availability is not a prerequisite for Claude execution authority.

## 2. Repair authority

Claude may diagnose and make the minimum repair necessary to restore behavior already required by controlling law. A repair must preserve the frozen architecture, mathematics, source identity, instrument scope, persistence rules, evidence rules, authority boundaries, and service/database/credential boundaries.

Claude may correct implementation defects, deployment drift, configuration drift, failed authorized migrations, startup failures, stale deployments, and other operational faults when the intended behavior is already unambiguously frozen.

A repair may not be used as a pretext to redesign or widen the system.

## 3. Merge and deployment authority

For work Claude owns or lawfully continues under this amendment, Claude may merge and deploy without a separate ChatGPT Pro approval when all controlling merge and deployment gates are satisfied.

Before merge:
- the final intended head must receive independent review from Codex, Copilot pull-request review, a qualified human reviewer, or the Owner;
- Claude may not be the sole independent reviewer of Claude-owned implementation;
- every required check must be green;
- every material P1/P2 finding must be resolved or explicitly dispositioned under controlling law; and
- no unresolved material review thread may remain.

After a lawful merge, Claude may perform migrations, environment changes, restarts, deployments, and activation steps already expressly authorized by the controlling technical freeze. No additional ChatGPT Pro approval is required solely because the workflow crossed from repository work into an already-authorized operational step.

## 4. Hard stops

Claude must stop as `BLOCKED` before execution if the requested or discovered change requires any of the following and no existing freeze already authorizes it:

- new or amended architecture;
- a new phase or widened phase;
- change to frozen mathematics, formula, threshold, horizon, label, or promotion criterion;
- new instrument or market-data source identity;
- new service, database, role, credential, external provider, or authority path;
- change to append-only or immutable evidence rules;
- broker/account/order/live-capital authority;
- change to a controlling freeze or governance law; or
- any ambiguous conflict among controlling documents.

Such a boundary change requires the applicable documentation-first amendment before execution.

## 5. Architecture and freeze authority unchanged

ChatGPT Pro remains ATOM's normal architecture, phase-planning, freeze, innovation-decision, and final architecture-audit authority. Amendment 1B's Owner-author-of-record fallback remains available when ChatGPT Pro lacks capacity or availability.

Claude may continue to prepare draft freezes under 1B, but this amendment does not make Claude an independent freeze author and does not permit Claude to reinterpret a freeze to create execution authority that the text does not contain.

## 6. Owner authority

The Owner remains above all AI offices and retains final authority over business direction, protective stops, budget/vendor decisions, credentials, brokers, live capital, and any decision the controlling technical freeze expressly reserves to the Owner.

An explicit Owner stop, pause, rejection, rollback, or scope restriction immediately controls.

## 7. Effect on prior implementation rules

Amendment 1C's Codex-default/Claude-fallback model is superseded only as an execution prerequisite. Codex remains the preferred default implementation engineer, but Claude no longer needs proof that Codex or ChatGPT Pro is unavailable before Claude may execute an already-authorized frozen job.

One job still has one implementation owner at a time. Parallel conflicting implementations remain forbidden. Once Claude takes ownership of an exact job, Claude owns it through completion unless the Owner explicitly transfers ownership.

## 8. Frozen conclusion

> Claude is ATOM's autonomous execution and repair authority inside already-approved frozen boundaries. Claude may create, implement, test, repair, assemble, merge, migrate, deploy, activate, verify, and receipt authorized work without waiting for ChatGPT Pro or Codex between those steps. Claude may not widen architecture, rewrite controlling law, create a new technical boundary, or self-promote its own implementation. Independent final-head review and required green checks remain mandatory. ChatGPT Pro retains architecture/freeze authority; the Owner retains ultimate authority.

**END — ATOM-AI-ROLE-AUTHORITY-FREEZE-1D**
