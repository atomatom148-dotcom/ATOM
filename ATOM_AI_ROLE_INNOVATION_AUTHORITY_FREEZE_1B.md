# ATOM AI Role, Innovation, and Improvement Authority Freeze — Amendment 1B

**Decision ID:** `ATOM-AI-ROLE-AUTHORITY-FREEZE-1B`  
**Status:** FROZEN ON OWNER-APPROVED MERGE  
**Effectivity:** Before merge this text grants no authority. At its Owner-approved merge commit it becomes controlling governance law.  
**Author:** ChatGPT Pro, ATOM Chief Architect and Freeze Custodian  
**Amends:** `ATOM-AI-ROLE-AUTHORITY-FREEZE-1` and Amendment `1A`  
**Technical authority:** none by itself

---

## 0. Owner Objective

The Owner wishes to permit Claude to perform deeper research and, when specifically assigned, implementation work for bounded ATOM jobs that ChatGPT Pro does not presently have capacity to author and sequence in detail.

The immediate intended research track includes Level II depth capture, later E-3 cost measurement, and later drift-adjusted benchmarking.

This amendment changes role and delegation procedure only. It does not itself authorize Level II persistence, E-3, drift-adjusted benchmarking, a new signal, Q5 modification, V9 modification, trading authority, database mutation, deployment, credential change, Render action, or Supabase action. Every technical change still requires its own controlling freeze or existing authorization.

---

## 1. Conditional Claude Implementation Authority

Base Law 2 remains controlling: one implementation job and one pull request have exactly one named implementation owner. Codex remains the default ATOM implementation owner.

For an individual job, Claude may act as the named implementation owner when either ChatGPT Pro or the Owner issues an explicit job card naming Claude as implementation owner beneath an already-controlling technical freeze.

The job card must identify the controlling freeze and decision ID, objective, exact repository/service/database/instrument/research scope, permitted actions, prohibited actions, required tests, required evidence, PASS/FAIL/INVALID/BLOCKED rules, stop condition, and required receipt.

Claude receives no general implementation authority from one assignment. One job remains one implementation owner.

---

## 2. Delegated Freeze Drafting

Claude may prepare proposed freeze, amendment, phase, preregistration, acceptance-gate, or research-contract text at the Owner's request. Claude's draft has zero controlling authority merely because Claude wrote it.

Ordinarily, a Claude-prepared draft becomes controlling law only when ChatGPT Pro reviews and adopts the text as architecture and freeze authority, ChatGPT Pro becomes author of record, required independent final-head review completes, every required check is green, the Owner approves it, and the documentation-only change is merged before dependent implementation begins.

### 2.1 Owner authorship fallback

Amendment 1A's sole-author continuity rule is narrowly amended as follows.

If ChatGPT Pro cannot presently author the required freeze or amendment because of capacity or availability, the Owner may explicitly adopt a Claude-prepared draft directly. When this occurs:

- the Owner is the author of record;
- Claude remains the draft preparer only;
- the decision must record that Owner authorship was used;
- all normal independent-review requirements remain;
- every required check must still be green;
- documentation-first ordering remains mandatory; and
- implementation may not begin until the Owner-approved document is merged.

This fallback does not grant Claude freeze authorship. Claude may never independently convert its own draft into controlling law.

---

## 3. Mandatory Independent Review When Claude Implements

Permanent Law 4 — No self-promotion — remains controlling.

When Claude is implementation owner, Claude may not be the sole final-head reviewer of that implementation. Before merge, the final intended head must receive independent review from at least one of Codex, Copilot's pull-request reviewer, a qualified human reviewer, or the Owner.

The reviewer must review the final intended head. An earlier review does not approve later commits. All required checks must be green. All material P1 and P2 findings must be resolved or explicitly dispositioned before merge.

Claude may analyze or explain its own work, but its own favorable assessment does not satisfy the independent-review requirement and cannot promote the work.

---

## 4. Authority Explicitly Unchanged

This amendment makes no change to:

- Owner final business authority;
- Owner final merge authority;
- Owner activation authority;
- Owner budget and vendor authority;
- Owner broker authority;
- Owner live-capital authority;
- documentation-first ordering;
- final-head independent review;
- every required check green with no waiver;
- minimal authorized scope;
- fail closed on ambiguity;
- preserve negative evidence;
- append-only evidence rules;
- preregistration before protected results are visible;
- deterministic evidence outranking AI opinion;
- one implementation owner per job and PR;
- no self-promotion;
- the mandatory innovation and promotion pipeline;
- existing broker boundaries;
- existing credential boundaries;
- existing Render authority;
- existing Supabase authority;
- existing database authority;
- existing market-data source restrictions; or
- existing technical freezes.

Claude implementation authority does not imply authority over credentials, external services, databases, migrations, deployment, brokers, capital, or infrastructure unless the controlling job card and technical freeze explicitly permit that action.

---

## 5. Research and Preregistration Boundary

Claude may perform exploratory read-only research and prepare proposed methodologies within an Owner-assigned research job.

Where results could influence a later promotion decision, the controlling research methodology, baseline, metrics, exclusions, comparison rule, and pass/fail criteria must still be preregistered before protected evaluation results are inspected.

Claude may not inspect results and then change the protected methodology to produce a favorable outcome. Informal exploratory findings must remain labeled informal and cannot be promoted into E-1, E-2, E-3, V9, family, simulator, or trading evidence without the appropriate controlling freeze.

---

## 6. Monitoring Boundary

Claude does not gain continuous-observation authority. When evidence requires monitoring between sessions or over long periods, monitoring must be performed by an authorized automated worker producing deterministic receipts.

Claude may inspect and interpret those receipts when invoked. Claude may not claim observation of periods it did not actually observe.

---

## 7. Effect on Amendment 1A

Amendment 1A remains controlling except for its sole-freeze-author continuity clause.

The statement that if ChatGPT Pro is unavailable every requested governance or technical boundary change remains BLOCKED until ChatGPT Pro authors the required amendment is superseded only to this extent:

> A boundary change normally waits for ChatGPT Pro authorship. If ChatGPT Pro lacks current capacity or availability, the Owner may explicitly adopt a prepared draft as author of record, subject to the same documentation-first merge, independent review, required-green-check, and scope requirements. Claude does not become freeze author through this path.

No other Amendment 1A provision is changed.

---

## 8. Frozen Conclusion

> Codex remains ATOM's default implementation engineer. Claude may become implementation owner only for an exact Owner- or ChatGPT-Pro-assigned job beneath controlling law. Claude may draft freezes but does not gain freeze authorship. Ordinarily ChatGPT Pro adopts and authors the controlling decision; when ChatGPT Pro lacks capacity or availability, the Owner may explicitly adopt the draft and becomes author of record. When Claude implements, an independent final-head reviewer is mandatory. No AI may promote its own work. Owner merge, activation, capital, infrastructure, credential, broker, and risk authority remain unchanged.

**END — ATOM-AI-ROLE-AUTHORITY-FREEZE-1B**
